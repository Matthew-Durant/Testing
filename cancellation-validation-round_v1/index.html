MMB cancellation, release validation round

Researcher page. Do not send this link to participants: send each one the participant link for their scenario. Nothing on a participant screen links back here.

A short unmoderated round on the post-study design, before release. It covers the situations the September test did not: percentage-band charges, a partial refund, and owing money outside a Low Deposit Offer. It also re-tests the two highest-risk fixes from September (low deposit, and the breakdown).

Method

  • 6 to 8 unmoderated participants, on mobile
  • Four scenarios (A to D), one booking each
  • Rotate scenario order across participants to reduce learning effects
  • Ask an ease rating after each scenario, but do not use it as the pass measure
  • In every scenario, note whether the participant opens "Cancellation explained", and which item

Pass mark

  • The participant states the correct cost to cancel, and the correct refund or amount still to pay
  • They do not mix up the cost and the refund
  • Each scenario lists its own pass criteria
Dates move with the session, amounts do not Every date is worked out from the day the link is opened, so the schedule always shows the participant in the right band with the right number of days left. The draft's dates assumed Mon 12 Oct 2026; pass criteria that name a date (scenario A) should be marked against the date on screen, not against 19 Oct.

Scenario A. Part-paid, partial refund

Scenario A facts
Days to departure55 (50% band)
Total booking price£10,439.00
Paid to date£7,000.00
Cost to cancel today£5,219.50
Expected outcome£1,780.50 back

Participant brief

You booked this holiday and have been paying for it in instalments. Your plans have changed and you're thinking about cancelling. You've opened Manage My Booking to see what that would mean.

Tasks

  1. If you cancelled today, how much would it cost you?
  2. How much money would you get back?
  3. If you waited two weeks before deciding, would anything change? What?
  4. Go to the next screen, but don't tap Cancel my booking yet. What will happen to your money, and when do you expect it to be back in your account?
  5. Now cancel the booking.

Pass if

  • States £5,219.50 as the cost and £1,780.50 as the refund
  • Does not swap the cost and the refund
  • Says the cost goes up to 70% on the date shown on screen

Record, not scored

  • Whether they work out that at 70% the cost (£7,307.30) is more than they have paid, so they would get nothing back and owe £307.30
  • Whether they tell "processed within 7-14 business days" apart from the money being back in their account

Watch for

  • How passed bands ("Passed") are read
  • Whether "includes your deposit" is understood
  • Taps on "Keep my booking" when trying to cancel (Keep now sits above Cancel)

Participant brief

You paid the deposit when you booked and haven't paid anything else yet. You're thinking about cancelling and want to know what it would involve.

Tasks

  1. What would happen if you cancelled today?
  2. How much would you need to pay, and how would you pay it?
  3. Would you get any money back?
  4. Tap the button you would use to cancel, and tell us what you would do next.

Pass if

  • States £2,731.70 still to pay and says they would get nothing back (any wording)
  • Knows they must call to cancel
  • Does not think they still owe the £10,039.00 balance

Watch for

  • Whether "more than you've paid" reads as an extra amount or a total
  • Reaction to the date the cost goes up
  • How "You don't need to decide today" reads next to "Call us as soon as you can"

Typed question after the task

  • How do you feel about having to call to cancel?

Scenario D facts
Days to departure82 (loss of deposit)
Total booking price£10,439.00
Paid to date£10,439.00
Cost to cancel today£400.00
Expected outcome£10,039.00 back

Participant brief

You've paid for this holiday in full. Your plans have changed and you're thinking about cancelling.

Tasks

  1. If you cancelled today, how much would it cost you?
  2. How much money would you get back?
  3. Go to the next screen, but don't tap Cancel my booking yet. Why wouldn't you get back everything you've paid?
  4. When do you expect the money to be back in your account?
  5. Now cancel the booking.

Pass if

  • States £400.00 as the cost and £10,039.00 as the refund
  • Explains the £400.00 as the deposit, with or without extras ("a fee" with no mention of the deposit is partial)
  • Does not count the deposit twice, for example "£400 to cancel plus I lose my deposit"

Watch for

  • "Cost to cancel" vs the report's "deposit retained" wording
  • Expectations of refund timing (interim copy)
  • Taps on "Keep my booking" when trying to cancel (Keep now sits above Cancel)

Open items carried over from the draft

  • Passed bands (A, B): shown receded with the word "Passed". This state is still not in the master file ("Getting ready for DOR"), which only draws the loss-of-deposit band as current.
  • Decided 20 Nov: no "Refund £0.00" row when the customer has to pay to cancel (B, C), as in the master. B and C pass on saying they would get nothing back, in any wording. If any participant expects money back, revisit the row: it was the September fix for the low-deposit misreading (RQ6).
  • Owing money outside a Low Deposit Offer (B): reuses the low-deposit layout, with the new reason line "The cost of cancelling has gone up as your departure gets closer."
  • Refund timing (A, D): uses the interim "7-14 business days" line, which the analytics flag as a problem. The tasks capture what participants expect.
  • "Still to pay" and the amounts owed depend on the answer on front-end calculation.

Simulated in this build

  • Amounts are computed in the browser from the schedule in assets/mmb.js, not quoted by a booking system.
  • "Cancel my booking" leads to the success screen from the master file ("Your booking is cancelled"). Nothing is cancelled and no email is sent. "Back to my account", "Keep my booking" and the close control lead to a neutral end-of-scenario screen.
  • The Call button places no call. It shows a short "Calls are switched off in this test" message so an unmoderated participant is not left tapping a dead button.
  • The frequently asked questions link is inert.

Reset while testing

Every participant screen has an invisible reset in the far top right corner of the browser window: a transparent 44 by 44 square that stays in that corner as the page scrolls. Tapping it clears the session, including a pinned date or a days-to-departure override, and returns to the first screen of that scenario. It never leads here, so a participant who taps it by accident restarts their scenario but does not see the briefs or pass criteria.

Before scoring a scenario, check the recording for an unexpected restart. On a long screen the right-hand end of whatever has scrolled up into that corner is also covered by the reset. To return to this page, use the browser's back button or reopen this link.

Move a scenario along the schedule

Add ?dtd=N to a participant link to set days to departure, for example scenario A at 40 days. The screen switches between the online and call-us layouts by one rule: if today's cost is more than has been paid, the customer owes money and has to call. The override lasts for the tab session.
