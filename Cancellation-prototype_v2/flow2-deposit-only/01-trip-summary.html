Frequently asked questions

Can I check in online?

Online check-in opens 14 days before your outbound flight and closes 4 hours before departure. You will find it under Documents once it opens.

Will I have to pay any fees to cancel my holiday?

Yes. Cancellation charges are based on how many days before your booked departure we receive your cancellation notice. Start the cancellation to see the exact figure that applies to your booking today.

How do I make a complaint about my holiday?

Speak to your holiday representative in resort first, as most issues can be resolved there. If that does not settle it, contact our customer service team when you return home.

Can you guarantee that my access requirements will be fulfilled?

Our ability to provide precise accessibility information and confirmation of services is entirely dependent upon notification of access needs by the customer. This must be done before booking, or as soon as possible after you have booked.

How do I cancel my excursion booking?

Excursions booked separately are cancelled separately. Cancelling your holiday here does not cancel them.

Your financial protection

All the flight-inclusive holidays on this website are financially protected by the ATOL scheme. When you pay you will be supplied with an ATOL Certificate. Please ask for it and check to ensure that everything you booked is listed on it. All non-flight package holidays on this website are financially protected under our ABTA bonding. This does not apply to other travel arrangements such as accommodation-only bookings.
